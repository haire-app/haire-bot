# Privacy Policy for hAIre

**Effective Date:** October 2026

hAIre ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect and use your information when using @hAIreBGBot on Telegram.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Telegram Details:** Telegram User ID, First Name, Last Name and Username (provided automatically by Telegram API).
- **Preferences:** Search criteria and job matching preferences provided via our Web Mini App.
- **Usage Data:** Job links clicked, viewed listings, and interactions within the Mini App to refine matching recommendations.

## 2. How We Use Your Data
- To filter scraped IT job listings and send daily matches to your chat.
- To analyze job interactions (such as viewed or clicked jobs) to improve match relevance and app quality.

## 3. Data Storage & Retention
- Your data is stored securely in our database.
- We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties.

## 4. Your Rights & Data Deletion
- You can update or delete your preferences at any time inside the Mini App.
- To completely erase your stored data from our database, contact support at `/help`.
